# Screen Contract — History

## Question

> Ini baru terjadi atau sudah menjadi pola?

## Visual form

A temporal narrative, not four monthly KPI boxes.

Show:

- current matched window
- three prior matched windows
- onset
- persistence
- recovery if present

## Default metric

Impressions.

Optional:

- Total Konten
- Total FYP
- FYP Rate

At most two normalized shapes when comparison helps.

Raw values remain accessible.

## Human reading

Example:

> Impressions mulai melemah sejak Agustus dan masih turun pada September. Sebelumnya relatif stabil.

## Output

- what changed
- when it began
- whether it persisted
- relationship to output if relevant
- evidence boundary
- directional cue to PIC decomposition
